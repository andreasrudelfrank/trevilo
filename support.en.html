Trevilo help

Your first trip

Tap “Get started” in the sample view, confirm the switch and enter your country, arrival and departure. Your own data is kept when switching to the sample view. Future trips belong in the planner; saved plans are independent scenarios.

Language

Trevilo starts in English on first launch. Open Settings to choose English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch or Swedish. Your choice is saved. Automatic optionally follows a supported device language and otherwise uses English. Your own place names, notes and document names stay unchanged. The app name is Trevilo in every language.

Understanding counters

Schengen counts unique calendar days, including entry and exit, within a rolling window. “To the limit” is the difference from today's count, not a guaranteed length of a future stay. Check specific dates in the planner. Tax counters are personal warning thresholds. FEIE counts confirmed full days abroad; travel boundaries, US stays and unknown days do not qualify automatically.

Backing up data

Overview > Export travel report > Full backup as JSON. This includes trips, settings, plans, document expiry dates, tasks and receipts. PDF is a readable report; CSV is a table. Only JSON restores the full collection. Files are not encrypted. Share them only with your intended recipients. Receipts: up to 5 MB per file and 20 MB in total; backup files up to 30 MB.

Reviewing imports

Calendar and photo data produce suggestions. Check dates and places before saving. Missing EXIF metadata, flight time zones and simplified country borders can prevent or affect recognition. Multiple detected countries or dates are not treated as a certain match. Image receipts support on-device OCR in the installed app. PDF receipts are kept unchanged.

App lock and widget

Profile > Enable app lock. The app uses your device's security; retain access to your device passcode. App lock does not replace encrypted backups. On iOS, the widget shows personal counters only after you enable them and while app lock is disabled. It shows a dated snapshot, updated when you open or save in the app.

Reminders and GPS

The relevant operating system permissions must be allowed. Location suggestions require review and are not automatically converted into actual stays. Android may restrict background work in power-saving modes; iOS may pause it after a force quit. Reminders use saved entries. Stay projections assume you remain in the last recorded country; they are not an entry eligibility decision.
